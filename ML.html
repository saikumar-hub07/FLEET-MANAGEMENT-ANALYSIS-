Fleet Management Analysis

A machine learning mini project. It predicts which vehicles will need maintenance in the next 30 days and groups the fleet into segments, all in your browser.

Training loss

Logistic regression trained with gradient descent. A falling curve means the model is learning.

What drives maintenance risk

Model weights on standardized features. Positive (amber) raises risk, negative (grey) lowers it.

20% of vehicles were held back and not used for training.
